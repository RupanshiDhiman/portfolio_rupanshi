Design Philosophy & Experience

Hi, I'm Rupanshi — a creative designer and aspiring researcher passionate about the intersection of art, technology, and human experience. My journey began in the world of fine arts and digital creativity, where I explored sketching, illustration, and storytelling through visual media.

Over the years, I expanded my skills into branding, social media strategy, and digital content creation, even working as a fashion influencer — an experience that taught me the power of audience engagement, identity-building, and emotional connection.

Currently, I am pursuing a Master's in Information Technology, where I have specialized in UI/UX design, digital media, and interactive technologies. I believe that design is not just about aesthetics but about creating experiences that truly resonate with people across cultures.

This belief fuels my research interests in Affective Computing in UX and Cross-Cultural UX Design — areas where I aim to explore how emotions, culture, and technology can be integrated to create more empathetic, inclusive, and meaningful digital interactions.

Alongside my academic studies, I have hands-on experience designing fashion brand websites, handling website management and content strategy, and creating digital illustrations and user-centered prototypes.
