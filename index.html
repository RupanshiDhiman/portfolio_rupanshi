About Me

I'm Rupanshi Dhiman a designer exploring the intersection of creativity, technology and user experience.

I’m a creative designer with a background in fine arts, branding, and UI/UX design. My journey started as a digital influencer, where I learned the importance of storytelling, visual identity, and connecting with audiences. This experience now fuels my passion for creating intuitive and engaging user experiences. Alongside managing brand websites and developing digital illustrations, I’m continuously exploring new ways to merge creativity with functionality in design.
